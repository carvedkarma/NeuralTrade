Problem: best_R is capped at tp_mult/sl_mult and TP hits return a single discrete R=1.3333, so auto-balance on r_min_enter cannot control the dominant TP-first mass. We need a continuous discriminator for TP-first bars.

   - t_hit (bars until hit, 1..horizon, or horizon if expiry)
   - mfe (max favorable excursion) over horizon, in price units
   - mae (max adverse excursion) over horizon, in price units
   These can be computed during the forward walk with rolling max/min.
