1️⃣ Fix data leakage (MOST IMPORTANT)

Problem (in your code):
	•	You fit scalers on the entire dataset
	•	Then split into train/val

This lets future information leak into training → fake accuracy.

Fix:
	1.	Split data by time first
	2.	Fit scalers only on training data
	3.	Apply the same scalers to validation/test

Impact:
	•	Accuracy may drop initially
	•	Signals become realistic and trustworthy
