ULTRA TRIGGER CONDITIONS (ALL must be true)

2) score >= rolling_percentile(score_history, ultra_score_pct)  (use existing rolling window, e.g. 500)
3) ADX >= ultra_adx_min
4) (side==LONG and edge_L >= ultra_edge_min) OR (side==SHORT and edge_S >= ultra_edge_min)
5) Regime aligned:
