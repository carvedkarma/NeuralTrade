5️⃣ How to turn your system into a TRUE strategy learner (step-by-step)
Step 1 — Add action labels to history (CRITICAL)

For each historical candle, simulate multiple actions:

Action	Definition
LONG	Enter at close, exit after N bars or SL/TP
SHORT	Same logic
HOLD	No trade

For each action, compute:

net PnL

MAE

MFE

time in trade

cost-adjusted expectancy

Now your dataset becomes:

(state, action) → reward


This is the single most important shift.

Step 2 — Train a policy model (this is real learning)

Instead of asking:

“What happened?”

Ask:

“Which action maximized reward given this state?”

This can be done with:

Gradient boosted trees

Neural networks

Or even logistic regression at first

The output should be:

P(long profitable)
P(short profitable)
P(hold optimal)


NOT just up/down probability.

Step 3 — Replace confidence with EXPECTED VALUE

Confidence should come from:

EV = Σ P(action) × reward(action)


This fixes:

confidence stuck at 50%

HOLD dominating accuracy

fake win rates

Your UI should show:

expected value

variance

risk-adjusted score

Step 4 — Make pattern memory action-aware

Instead of:

“Pattern had 60% win rate”

Track:

win rate per action

avg reward per action

drawdown per action

Patterns become:

“In this state, SHORT outperformed LONG by 3×”

That’s strategy knowledge.
