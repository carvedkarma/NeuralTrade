1) Train on tradable targets, not just returns

Instead of predicting raw future return y, predict net edge after costs:

edge_H = log(close[t+H]/close[t]) − (fee + slippage + spread_est)

Then your decision rule is clean:

trade only if edge_H > 0

This alone improves “precision” because the model learns what’s actually profitable.

2) Predict uncertainty (so the bot knows when NOT to trade)

Add quantiles (q10/q50/q90) or predict mean+variance.

Why it matters:

you can filter low-quality predictions

size positions based on confidence

reduce overtrading (biggest bot killer)

A simple implementation:

output mu and log_sigma

loss = Gaussian NLL (or quantile loss)

3) Proper walk-forward validation + threshold tuning

Most people train once and pick a threshold randomly.

Better:

do rolling windows (train → val → test)

for each window, tune:

entry threshold

stop/trailing rules

cooldown settings

Your “precision” comes as much from decision layer tuning as from the model.

4) Sample weighting (fix the “mostly noise” problem)

Crypto minute data is ~90% chop.

If you train uniformly, the model learns “predict 0 return” and looks good on MSE but is useless.

Fix:

weight samples by |future_return| or by volatility regime

or oversample rare “move” buckets

keep chronology (no random shuffling across time blocks)

This increases signal-to-noise and improves edge detection.

5) Regime-aware training (separate brains)

One model trying to handle:

trend

range

panic
usually becomes average at all.

Two good approaches:
A) Mixture-of-experts (best)

a small “gating network” predicts regime

experts specialize (trend expert, range expert, high-vol expert)

B) Two-model system (simpler)

model1: regime classifier

model2: return predictor conditioned on regime

This increases precision a lot, especially for automated trading.

6) Add market microstructure proxies (still from candles)

Even without order book, you can add strong proxies:

spread proxy: (high-low)/close

liquidity proxy: volume / (high-low)

volatility-of-volatility

wick/body ratios (liquidation-like spikes)

“impulse vs grind” features (price change per volume)

These features help the model stop getting fooled by fakeouts.

The biggest precision boost (and it’s not ML)
Build the “no-trade” filter as a first-class objective

Train a second head that predicts:

P(trade-worthy) (binary)
Labels:

1 if abs(edge_H) > threshold AND move is “clean”

0 otherwise

Then your bot is:

trade filter says “allowed?”

direction/edge head decides long/short + size

This increases live precision dramatically.
